## STM32 Nucleo-G431KB Pin Mapping
| Physical Pin | Logical Pin | Logical Function                                |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| 2            | PA10        | Etch Signal Out (DAC)                           |
| 1            | PA9         | Comparator Threshold (DAC)                      |
| 12           | PA8         | Buffered Comparator Out (ADC/GPIO)              |
| 19           | PA0         | Resistive (Transimpedance) Measurement In (ADC) |
| 20           | PA1         | Differential Measurement In (ADC)               |
